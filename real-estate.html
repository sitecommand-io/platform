💡 70% Rule
The 70% rule says:
Max Offer = (ARV × 0.70) − Rehab Cost

This ensures enough profit margin after buying, rehabbing, holding costs, and selling.

Example:
ARV = $250,000
Rehab = $40,000
Max Offer = ($250k × 0.70) − $40k = $135,000
