Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any technical skills to use SiteCommand? +
No technical skills needed at all. We build and set up everything for you. You just fill out a form with your company info and we handle the rest. If you can use a smartphone you can use SiteCommand.
How long does setup take? +
Your complete system is ready within 24 hours of signing up. We build your branded website, set up your portal, configure your crew app, and deliver everything to your email with a simple guide.
Can I use my existing phone number for the AI assistant? +
Yes — we can forward calls from your existing number to the AI. Or we provide you with a new local business number. Either way works perfectly.
What happens after the 14-day free trial? +
After 14 days you are charged your monthly plan fee. We send a reminder email 3 days before the trial ends. If you cancel before the trial ends you are never charged anything.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes — cancel anytime with no fees or penalties. Your account stays active until the end of your current billing period. We do not do long-term contracts.
Is my data secure? +
Yes. All data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud) with enterprise-grade security. Your client data, project info, and business data are never shared or sold. Each contractor has their own isolated database.
Does the permit agent work outside Delaware and Pennsylvania? +
Currently the permit agent covers all of Delaware and greater Philadelphia/Pennsylvania suburbs. We are expanding to more states. Elite plan subscribers get new states added automatically at no extra cost.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? +
Yes — upgrade or downgrade anytime from your account dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
