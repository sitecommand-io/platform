Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Ali Signature Construction LLC ("we", "us") operates SiteCommand. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email, phone, password (encrypted)
  • Business information: company name, address, services, brand color
  • Project data: client names, project details, photos, expenses
  • Call data: AI phone call transcripts and lead information
  • Payment information: processed by Stripe — we never store card numbers

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the SiteCommand service
  • To send you system notifications and updates
  • To provide customer support
  • To process payments through Stripe

Data Storage

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Firebase.google.com), a Google Cloud service with enterprise-grade security. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data to any third party. We share data only with:

  • Google Firebase (data storage)
  • Stripe (payment processing)
  • Twilio (SMS and phone services)
  • Google Gemini AI (AI features — no personally identifiable data shared)

Your Rights

You may request a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of your account and associated data by emailing support@sitecommand.com.

Cookies

We use browser localStorage to save your preferences and session data. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS, encrypted passwords, and Firebase security rules. No system is 100% secure — we will notify you promptly of any breach affecting your data.
