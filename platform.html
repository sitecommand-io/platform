Connect Your Own Domain

Want smithroofing.com instead of smithroofing.sitecommand.io? Enter your domain below and follow the 3-step DNS instructions. Takes 10 minutes to set up, 24-48 hours to fully propagate.

DNS Setup Instructions (do this at your domain registrar — GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.):
TypeNameValueTTL
CNAME www proxy.sitecommand.io Auto
A @ 76.76.21.21 Auto
Step by step:
1. Log in to your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google Domains, etc.)
2. Go to DNS Settings for your domain
3. Add the CNAME record above for "www"
4. Add the A record above for "@" (root domain)
5. Wait 24-48 hours for DNS to propagate worldwide
6. Your site will automatically appear at your domain — no other action needed
⏳ Pending — DNS changes can take 24-48 hours to propagate. Your subdomain still works during this time.
Good to know: Both your subdomain and your custom domain work at the same time. You never lose access to smithroofing.sitecommand.io even after connecting smithroofing.com. Share either link — they both go to the same website.
